## 1. Executive Overview

This project focused on developing a data-driven intelligence system to analyze mobile data consumption behavior, predict user-level usage, and identify revenue optimization opportunities.

The solution combines:
- Predictive modeling
- Behavioral segmentation
- Business intelligence insights

The outcome is a system that enables more accurate pricing strategies, improved network planning, and targeted bundle offerings.

---

## 2. Business Problem

SA Telecom lacked granular visibility into how users consume mobile data across applications, time periods, and demographic groups.

This resulted in:
- Inefficient bundle pricing
- Poor segmentation of customers
- Missed revenue opportunities
- Suboptimal network utilization
