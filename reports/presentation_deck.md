# Slide 2 — Business Problem

## The Business Challenge

Telecom providers need to understand:

- Who consumes the most data?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- Which services drive consumption?
- Which plans are most profitable?
- When does network demand increase?
- How accurately can future usage be forecast?

### Business Goal

Use customer consumption data to improve:

**Revenue → Customer Targeting → Bundles → Network Planning**
